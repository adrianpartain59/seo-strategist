# Template: list guide (checklist, template, shopping or packing list)

Linkable, shareable assets; chosen for shareability as much as volume.
9 sections, ~36 H3s, ~1,050–1,150 words.

## Section order

| # | Section | H3s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | H1 + the printable/savable artifact linked right under it ("Print the List", "Save to Phone") | 1 |
| 2 | The whole list on one screen ("The Checklist at a Glance", grouped by time or by aisle/category) | 4 |
| 3–6 | One H2 per group, 4 H3 items each (Before you start / Week by week; or aisle by aisle) | 4 each |
| 7 | One safety or "what to skip / what not to worry about" section | 4 |
| 8 | Product section + next steps (calculators, sibling guides) | 4 |
| 9 | FAQ (7), ending with the pricing question | 7 |

## Rules

- No email gate on the printable. Specify it as Key Components: "Printable
  one-page list grouped by <x>, linked under the H1", "checkable cards that can
  be printed or saved to the phone", Print/Save buttons.
- Each item: what to do + why, in the 2-sentence rhythm.
- Title `<Keyword>: <benefit phrase> | <Brand>`, 45–60 chars. Slug may be
  descriptive (`/what-to-track-first-month-on-x`).
- Schema: FAQPage + WebPage (`about` the topic) + Organization.
