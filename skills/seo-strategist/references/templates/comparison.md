# Template: comparison page ("A vs B", "switching from A to B", "<X> alternative")

9 blocks (H1 + 8 H2), 36–39 H3s, ~1,200–1,450 words. Neutral, sourced,
decision left to the reader (or their professional).

## Classify the pair first

- **Same thing, different packaging** (one ingredient, two labels; one engine,
  two plans): angle = why two exist.
- **Same job, different things** (two drugs for one use; two apps for one
  job): angle = the evidence, head to head.
- **Category vs category**: angle = a map of which products belong to each,
  plus all the evidence.
Sibling vs pages each own a different differentiator in title and H1.

## "A vs B" order

| # | Block | H3s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | H1 with a sourced, neutral lede. Regulated pairs (drugs, financial products): "Nothing here picks a winner, because that choice belongs with you and <your professional>." Product pairs (the client vs a competitor, two tools): a fit verdict instead, "Pick <A> if…, pick <B> if…", with the competitor's real strengths named (awards, platforms, features it has and the client lacks) | 1 |
| 2 | The Short Answer: 4 standalone takeaways (product pairs: the fit verdict box first) | 4 |
| 3 | "<A> and <B> at a Glance": a two-column table (5–7 rows, one fact per row, citation per column) + nuance H3s | 4–5 |
| 4 | Head-to-head evidence, only if a direct comparison exists, always with "What the <Study/Test> Does Not Tell You" | 4 |
| 5 | Specs/steps/plans quoted from each source separately + "No Conversion Between Them" where conversions would be advice | 4 |
| 6 | Downsides/side effects/limits per source + "Why the Numbers Are Not a Fair Head-to-Head" | 4–5 |
| 7 | Shared risks or shared requirements | 4 |
| 8 | Product block: "<Track/Use/Manage> Either <A or B> in <Brand>" (each H3 starts with the brand). If no direct evidence exists, block 4 becomes "Questions to Bring to <Professional>" here and the product block moves up | 4–5 |
| 9 | FAQ (7); Q7 = "Can <Brand> <do X> with <A> or <B>?" | 7 |

## Product pairs (the client vs a competitor, two tools)

Use this order instead of the one above when neither item is regulated:
H1 with the fit-verdict lede → Short Answer (verdict box: "Pick <A> if…, pick
<B> if…", then 3 takeaways) → "<A> and <B> at a Glance" table from each
company's own pages, dated → feature by feature, one H3 per job the reader
needs done → pricing compared from the official pricing pages, dated, with the
billing period stated → "Where <Competitor> Is the Better Fit" (its real
strengths) → switching or migration steps → product block → FAQ (7).

## "Switching from A to B" order

H1 (who decides) → Short Answer (takeaway 1 is always the hard limit, e.g. "There
is no one-to-one conversion") → What changes → what each source says about its
own starting point + "What the Sources Leave to <Professional>" → What to track
through the switch → what to watch for on B + "Signs That Need Attention" →
questions to ask + the product's report → set up B in the product → FAQ (7).
Switching pages are action- and record-oriented; the product appears in 3–4
blocks.

## "<Competitor> alternative"

H1 "<Competitor> Alternative: <who it suits>" → Short answer (what each is best
for, honestly) → at-a-glance table from each company's own listing, dated →
where the client differs → who should stay with the competitor → switching
steps (data import/export) → reviews → FAQ. No criticism, no prices, "details
checked <Month YYYY>".

## Rules

- Every fact attributed to a named document with its date; numbers with their
  comparator and population.
- Never rank, convert or recommend; route the decision to the reader or their
  professional.
- Cross-links: vs ↔ switching (via one-line FAQ answers), vs ↔ sibling vs
  (glance H3 + FAQ), vs → each item's deep guide ("Full Breakdowns for Each
  <Item>" H3), and the product's entity pages and calculators where relevant.
- Title `<A> vs <B>: <differentiator>`, 45–60 chars, no brand suffix; switching
  `Switching From <A> to <B>: What to Know`. H1 is a different angle phrase, ≤67
  chars. Meta 140–160 chars opening with a contrast ("Both contain X, yet…") or
  the objection answer ("Your data moves over in one import.").
- Hero Key Components: source line with revision dates, disclaimer +
  not-affiliated note, jump links. A printable question list next to a preview
  of the product's report is a good take-away.
- Schema: FAQPage + WebPage (or MedicalWebPage with `about` = both entities)
  with `lastReviewed` + Organization.
