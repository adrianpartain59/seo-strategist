# Template: audience guide (persona, life stage, condition, role)

For informational "<topic> and <audience>" questions a segment asks AI
assistants. A commercial "<category> app for <audience>" search is an Audience
page and uses `landing-page.md`, with the audience's problems in section 2 and
any limits line stated once. For the questions a segment asks AI assistants
("which planner works with ADHD", "period tracker for perimenopause"). Low volume, high
intent, high AI-answer value. 9 sections, ~35 H3s, ~1,100–1,250 words.

## Section order

| # | Section | H3s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | H1 hero. The H3 states the honest status first ("<Audience> Is Not an Approved/Official Use…", "Some of This Is Documented, and Some Is Not"), then what the page covers | 1 |
| 2 | Short answer: 4 facts. Item 1 = the honest status; last item = who decides / what tells you more over time | 4 |
| 3 | What each primary source says about this audience, one H3 per source or product ("Quoting each source separately avoids blending different statements into one claim") + a closing "Where <Audience> Fits" H3 | 4–5 |
| 4 | Second topic the audience must know (a risk, an interaction, a constraint) | 4 |
| 5 | The overlap or confusion ("<Audience Change> or <Product Effect>? Sorting Out the Overlap") | 4 |
| 6 | What to track/log, each H3 mapped to a product feature | 4 |
| 7 | Questions to bring to the professional, or the product section | 4 |
| 8 | Share the record + related guides for other audiences | 4 |
| 9 | FAQ (7), ending with the pricing question in the approved wording | 7 |

## Rules

- No claim that the product or topic helps the audience beyond what a source
  says. Off-label or unofficial uses are "not a listed use" plus "your
  <professional> decides".
- Life-stage and audience pages link to each other, to the pillar guide and to
  the feature pages they mention.
- Title `<Keyword>: <angle> | <Brand>`, 45–60 chars; meta (140–160) opens with a question
  or tension hook, then "covers what <sources> say … and what to ask".
- Schema: FAQPage + WebPage/MedicalWebPage (`about` = the audience/condition)
  with `lastReviewed` + Organization.
